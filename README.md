# 🏅 Olympic Medals Analysis (1900 – 2008)

An interactive **Power BI** dashboard exploring over a century of **Olympic Games medal history (1900 – 2008)**. This project transforms historical sports records into clear visual insights to track national dominance, athlete achievements, gender representation, and medal distributions across sports and disciplines.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview
Analyzing multi-decade Olympic datasets reveals long-term athletic trends, geopolitical shifts, and evolving participation across countries. The objective of this project is to:
* Track total **Gold, Silver, and Bronze medals** awarded across Olympic editions from **1900 to 2008**.
* Compare country-level athletic dominance and host city performance over time.
* Analyze medal distributions across different **sports, disciplines, and gender categories**.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, interactive dashboard design, and visual storytelling.
* **Power Query (M):** Data cleaning, formatting, and ETL transformations.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom measures for Total Medals, Gold/Silver/Bronze counts, Country Rankings, and Gender Share %.

---

## 🔄 Data Preprocessing & Modeling
To prepare the century-long dataset for smooth, multi-dimensional exploration in Power BI, the following steps were executed:
1. **Data Cleaning & Standardization:** Standardized country codes/names, sport categories, and medal labels while handling missing entries using **Power Query**.
2. **Data Modeling:** Structured relationships across editions, countries, sports, and athletes to enable fast cross-filtering.
3. **KPI & Measure Development:** Built dynamic **DAX** measures to calculate total medal tallies, medal-type breakdowns, and top-performing athletes and nations.

---

## 📈 Key Analytical Highlights & Insights
* **Country Dominance:** Identified top medal-winning nations across the 108-year timeline and evaluated their strongest sporting disciplines.
* **Medal Breakdown:** Compared Gold, Silver, and Bronze distributions across individual and team sports.
* **Evolution & Participation:** Tracked the expansion of Olympic events and the steady growth of female athlete participation from 1900 to 2008.
